# We're back on Yahoo — what changes for 2026

**Read time: 60 seconds.** Draft is **Sunday Aug 30, 9:30pm ET**.

We're returning to our original Yahoo league — the same one we started in, with its
settings still intact. I diffed our Sleeper scoring against it category by category.

**31 of 36 categories are identical.** Half-PPR, 6-point passing TDs, every field goal
tier, every points-allowed band, all the defensive categories — unchanged.

Three things are genuinely different:

### 1. Interceptions thrown: -1 → **-2**

Sleeper had these at -1. Our original Yahoo league has always had -2 (Yahoo's own
default is -1, so this was a deliberate choice back when we set the league up). We're
going with -2, the long-standing rule.

**Draft impact:** turnover-prone QBs are slightly less valuable than your Sleeper
rankings suggest.

### 2. 50+ yard field goals all pay **5**

Sleeper paid 5 for a 50-59 yarder and 6 for a 60+. Yahoo only has one "50+" bucket, so
it can't tell the two apart. Everything 50 and over pays 5.

**Impact:** costs 1 point on 60-yard kicks. There were a handful league-wide all last
season.

### 3. No more points for forced fumbles

Sleeper gave 1 point for forcing a fumble. Yahoo has no such stat — nothing to turn on.

**Impact:** fumble *recoveries* still pay 2, same as before, so a strip-and-recover play
scores exactly as it did. Only a strip that the other team falls on goes unrewarded.

Everything else — 12 teams, the same starting lineup (QB/RB/RB/WR/WR/TE/FLEX/K/DEF,
